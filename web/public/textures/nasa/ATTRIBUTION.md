# NASA texture attribution

The textures in this folder are sourced from NASA's public NASA-3D-Resources repository:

- `earth.jpg` - `Images and Textures/Earth (A)/Earth (A).jpg`
- `venus.jpg` - `Images and Textures/Venus/Venus.jpg`
- `mars.jpg` - `Images and Textures/Mars/Mars.jpg`
- `jupiter.jpg` - `Images and Textures/Jupiter/Jupiter.jpg`
- `saturn.jpg` - `Images and Textures/Saturn/Saturn.jpg`
- `neptune.jpg` - `Images and Textures/Neptune/Neptune.jpg`

Source: https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources

NASA states that these resources are free to download and use. Follow the NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines when redistributing them.

## Jupiter, Cassini global map

- `jupiter-cassini.webp` - NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute, "Cassini's Best Maps of Jupiter"
  (PIA07782), cylindrical map assembled from Cassini narrow-angle camera images taken in
  December 2000. Re-encoded as WebP at 3600 x 1800; the rows beyond its southern edge, where
  the map has no data, are filled with the mean colour (the game paints Jupiter's poles as
  Juno saw them).

Source: https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA07782
