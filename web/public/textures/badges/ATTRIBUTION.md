# World badges

128 x 64 thumbnails for the HUD's chapter card, scaled down from the maps already in this
project (`../nasa/` and `../expedition/`, credited in their own ATTRIBUTION.md files). Venus,
Uranus, Europa, Titan, Triton and Uranus's moons have no map here, and seen from space they are
nearly featureless (cloud, haze or ice), so their badges are a flat tint of the real colour.
