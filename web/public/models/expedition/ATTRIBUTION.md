# Expedition models and pictures

Made for this portfolio by its owner with Higgsfield (28 September 2026), then
prepared for the web: the models with glTF Transform (meshopt geometry
compression, WebP textures at 2048 px for the ship and 1024 px for everything
else), the pictures with sharp.

## Trees (`public/models/expedition/tree-*.glb`)

Made for this game by its owner in Blender 4.2 (5 November 2026) with the
script in `source_assets/trees/maketree2.py`: trunks, limbs, branches and
twigs as bark tubes grown by rules taken from photographs of real trees, and
foliage as cut-out photographs of leaf sprays clumped at the twigs, darker
inside the crown (vertex colours, read by the game as occlusion). The bark and
leaf pictures were generated with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.6) as cut-outs on
flat magenta and keyed with `source_assets/trees/textures.py`.

## Food plants (`public/models/expedition/forage-*.glb`, 8 November 2026)

Made for this game by its owner with `source_assets/forage` (Blender 5.2 via `bpy`,
Python with Pillow and NumPy): every stem, leaf blade and fruit is modelled from
the botany of the real plant, and every picture (leaf outlines, veins, fruit
skins, bark) is drawn by `pictures.py`, no photographs or third-party images.

| File | Plant | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `forage-currant.glb` | Redcurrant (Ribes rubrum): arching canes, lobed leaves, strings of red berries | Earth, Haven |
| `forage-melon.glb` | Melon vine (Cucumis melo) with golden netted melons | desert worlds |
| `forage-opuntia.glb` | Prickly pear (Opuntia) with red fruit on the pads' rims | Mars's canyons |
| `forage-rosette.glb` | A succulent rosette with its offsets and a stalk of seed pods (invented) | the Moon, Mercury |
| `forage-frostberry.glb` | A low evergreen shrub with rimed leaves and pale blue berries (invented) | frozen moons |
| `forage-icekelp.glb` | Strap fronds over a cluster of translucent bulbs (invented) | glacier moons |

The rocks (`rock-*.glb`) were simplified from Tripo's 1.4 million triangles to
two levels of detail sharing one set of vertices (nodes `lod0` and `lod1`),
keeping the colour and normal pictures; the game recolours each to its world.

The animals (and the alien walkers) are still meshes; `components/expedition/creatures.ts`
measures their legs, neck and tail when they load and walks them in the vertex shader.

## The explorer's motions (8 Nov 2026)

The clips in `astronaut.glb` were carried onto the explorer's own skeleton (the mesh, skin and rig are
unchanged) by `source_assets/astronaut` (its README has every command), from two sources:

- **CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database** (mocap.cs.cmu.edu), in the BVH conversion by Bruce
  Hahne (cgspeed.com), as mirrored at github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap (commit 09a07f5). CMU: "Use
  this data! This data is free for use in research and commercial projects worldwide." It may be used
  in products that are sold, but the data itself must not be resold. Credit, as CMU asks: *The data
  used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from
  NSF EIA-0196217.*
- **Quaternius, Universal Animation Library** (standard, free version, quaternius.com), CC0 1.0
  (public domain), as distributed on itch.io and mirrored at
  github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library (commit e24c23c; its LICENSE is CC0 1.0).

| Clip | What it is | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `walk` | Walking, 1.2 m/s | CMU 35_01 (subject 35, walk) |
| `walk-brisk` | A brisk walk, 1.7 m/s | CMU 132_17 (subject 132, walk fast) |
| `jog` | Jogging, 3.0 m/s | CMU 35_17 (subject 35, run/jog) |
| `run` | Running, 4.1 m/s | CMU 16_46 (subject 16, run/jog) |
| `sprint` | Sprinting, 7.7 m/s | Quaternius UAL `Sprint_Loop` |
| `lope` | The lunar lope: the jog retimed for the Moon's gravity (long floating bounds) | CMU 35_17, retimed |
| `idle` | Standing, weight settling from foot to foot | CMU 77_02 (subject 77, standing) |
| `idle-shift` | Shifting weight, settling the stance | CMU 77_02 (later in the take) |
| `idle-stretch` | A stretch | CMU 141_13 (subject 141, stretch and yawn) |
| `idle-wrist` | A glance at the wrist display | CMU 77_02, the arm and head keyed over it |
| `jump` | A standing jump: crouch, spring, landing | CMU 16_01 (subject 16, jump) |
| `land-heavy` | Landing hard after a drop, knees giving | CMU 82_02 (subject 82, jump off a ledge) |
| `pickup` | Reaching out to take something | Quaternius UAL `Interact` |
| `swim` | Front crawl | CMU 126_10 (subject 126, free style) |
| `tread` | Treading water | Quaternius UAL `Swim_Idle_Loop` |

Speeds are at the explorer's size in the game (1.9 m). `fall`, `grab`, `hang`, `climb`, `eat`, `rest`
and `knockdown` are still the clips Meshy made with the model.
