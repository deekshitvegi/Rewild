# Rewild

A space game. Three hundred years after a superflare emptied Earth, four crew come home from Haven, a colony by the
black hole TON 618, to bring it back to life, and find it held by the Harrow, a species that strips dead worlds.
Win it back in the cockpit, aboard their ships and on the ground; build bases, a fleet and finally living cities
from what you win.
