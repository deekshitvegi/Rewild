## Content rules

- Keep one primary portfolio purpose per planet.
- Use moons for individual schools, roles, projects, research programs, interests, client websites, and verified certificates.
- Do not duplicate full professional profiles on Saturn; LinkedIn, GitHub, and Hugging Face remain on the Sun.
- Keep client descriptions factual. Only the two publicly available client websites are linked; private and pre-launch work remains unlinked.
- Add certification names, dates, verification links, and claims only when they can be verified from a public credential or the owner's profile.
- Social and gaming handles supplied by the owner may be displayed as text; only verified public profile URLs should be clickable.
